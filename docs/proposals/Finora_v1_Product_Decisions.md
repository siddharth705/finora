# Finora v1 Product Decisions

**Document Type:** Product Decision Record
**Status:** Approved
**Scope:** Finora v1 Public Launch Strategy
**Company:** Finora Intelligence LLP

---

## 1. Product Vision

**Positioning: Personal Finance Operating System**

Finora is not an expense tracker. It is a complete financial control platform that helps users:

1. Track money
2. Understand money
3. Improve money
4. Build wealth

---

# 2. Market & Launch Decisions

## Market

**Decision:** India only for v1

Supported:

- INR currency
- Indian users
- Indian financial behavior
- Indian bank statements
- Indian merchants

Deferred:

- International currencies
- Global banking
- Multi-country compliance

---

## Launch Type

**Decision:** Full public launch

Finora v1 launches as a complete finance platform.

---

## Launch Date Strategy

**Decision:** Readiness-based launch

Launch only when:

- Product is stable
- Legal requirements are complete
- Store readiness is complete
- Operations are ready
