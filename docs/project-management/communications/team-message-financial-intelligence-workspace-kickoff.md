🚀 Project Kickoff: Financial Intelligence Workspace

Hi Team,

Congratulations on successfully completing and stabilizing the Financial Intelligence Engine v1. 🎉

With all planned tasks completed and the final architectural issues resolved, we're officially freezing this phase.

Our next objective is not to start Financial Evidence or Gmail integration yet.

Instead, our focus is to build a Financial Intelligence Workspace—a centralized management portal that gives complete visibility into the intelligence we've already built.

The goal is to make every decision taken by Finora transparent, explainable, and manageable through a professional interface.

## Objective

The Financial Intelligence Workspace will become the operational center of Finora.

It should provide users with the ability to manage and monitor:

* Merchant Intelligence
* Rule Engine
* Learning Engine
* Relationship Engine
* Reconciliation
* Duplicate Detection
* Recurring Detection
* Analytics
* Statement Imports
* System Configuration

This phase is about polishing, managing, and visualizing the Financial Intelligence Engine—not introducing new intelligence.

## Important Scope

This milestone is focused only on the Financial Intelligence Workspace.

The following initiatives are intentionally out of scope for this phase:

* Financial Evidence Platform
* Gmail Integration
* PDF Receipt Processing
* OCR
* SMS Parsing
* Financial Knowledge Graph
* AI Copilot

These will be addressed in the next major milestone after the workspace is complete.

## Technical Expectations

* Reuse existing APIs wherever possible.
* Follow our established layered architecture.
* Build reusable React components.
* Maintain a consistent design language.
* Ensure responsive layouts and excellent UX.
* Include appropriate testing and documentation for all new components.

## Goal

By the end of this milestone, Finora should have a polished Financial Intelligence Workspace that provides complete visibility and management over the Financial Intelligence Engine.

Once this workspace is complete, we'll begin the next architectural phase: Financial Evidence, which will introduce external evidence sources such as Gmail, PDFs, and future integrations.

Let's focus on delivering a professional, scalable, and intuitive management experience before moving on to the next major capability.

Let's get started! 🚀
